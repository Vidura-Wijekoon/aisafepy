We organize related work along five axes and use the capability
matrix in Table~\ref{tab:capabilities} to position AIsafePy.

\subsection{Content-classifier guardrails}

The dominant class of open-source guardrail is the content
classifier. Llama Guard 4 \citep{llamaguard}, ShieldGemma
\citep{shieldgemma}, Llama Prompt Guard 2 \citep{promptguard},
Qwen3Guard-Stream, OmniGuard, and Nemotron-3-Content-Safety all sit
here. They differ in model size and modality but share a uniform
interface: consume a prompt or response, emit a category and a
probability. Commercial APIs (OpenAI Moderation, Azure AI Content
Safety, AWS Bedrock Guardrails, Lakera Guard) follow the same shape.
These tools work well as part of a defense-in-depth stack but face a
structural ceiling on indirect prompt injection, as shown
empirically by AgentDojo \citep{debenedetti2024agentdojo}.

\subsection{DSL and decorator guardrails}

NVIDIA's NeMo Guardrails \citep{nemoguardrails} introduced Colang, a
DSL for dialogue rails. Guardrails AI \citep{guardrails} popularized
a Python-decorator validator hub with roughly 60 community
validators. OpenAI's Agents SDK added input and output guardrail
hooks with tripwire exceptions. llm-guard \citep{llmguard} ships 35
input and output scanners. The market trajectory between Colang and
Pythonic decorators has settled in favor of decorators, motivating
AIsafePy's choice not to introduce a new DSL.

\subsection{Capability-based information-flow control}

A research line from Google DeepMind formalizes indirect injection
as an information-flow problem. CaMeL \citep{camel2025} introduces
a privileged-LLM / quarantined-LLM split plus a Python-DSL
interpreter that propagates labels (provenance, capability,
integrity) through tool calls; a tool whose arguments carry an
UNTRUSTED label is rejected by construction. CaMeL is presented
as a design pattern and a research prototype rather than a packaged
library; LlamaFirewall \citep{llamafirewall} includes a CoT auditor
but no full IFC pass. AIsafePy.flow is, to our knowledge, the first
OSS package to bring CaMeL-style capability-based IFC to OpenAI
Agents SDK, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, Anthropic tool-use, and MCP.

\subsection{Streaming detection}

Streaming guardrails are an emerging design space. NVIDIA NeMo's
stream mode buffers chunks; the OpenAI Agents SDK runs guards in
parallel and races against generation, but documents that violative
tokens may briefly appear. The deployed pattern in voice and
IDE-assistant products is to wrap a content classifier with custom
chunking logic per application. No general guardrail library
packages a tiered, forecasting-style cascade with an explicit p95
latency budget and a uniform Guard primitive across tiers.
AIsafePy.stream provides that primitive and ships pluggable
forecaster, classifier, and probe tiers behind a single
\texttt{GuardPipeline} interface.

\subsection{Activation probes and steering}

Anthropic's \textit{Simple probes can catch sleeper agents}
\citep{anthropicprobes} demonstrates that residual-stream linear
probes near-perfectly distinguish defection in trained backdoor
models. Constitutional Classifiers \citep{sharma2025constclass}
extends the probe-plus-cascade pattern to production-scale
jailbreak defense at low refusal rates. Activation steering as a
research technique is established by Turner et al.
\citep{turner2023steering}, the contrastive activation addition
recipe \citep{panickssery2023cad}, and the broader representation
engineering programme of Zou et al. \citep{zou2023repe}, with
open-source implementations such as repeng \citep{repeng}.
AIsafePy.stream embeds linear and MLP probes as a first-class
Tier 3, and AIsafePy.adapt's steering-vector target operationalizes
the contrastive recipe end-to-end.

\subsection{Red-team and evaluation harnesses}

PyRIT \citep{pyrit}, Garak \citep{garak}, Promptfoo, DeepEval, and
Inspect AI \citep{inspectai} produce JSONL logs of attacks and
scored outcomes. None of them automates the round-trip back into the
runtime stack. Galileo Luna-2 and Lakera Red$\rightarrow$Guard sell
this automation commercially; the underlying techniques (cluster
embeddings, classifier distillation, regex synthesis, policy
emission, steering training) are all individually published. The
gap is an integration gap, which AIsafePy.adapt fills.
