We presented AIsafePy, an Apache-2.0 Python library that targets
three high-leverage gaps in the open-source LLM-agent safety
ecosystem: capability-based information-flow control around
tool-using agents, streaming-native cascaded guardrails with optional
white-box activation probes, and a continuous eval-to-guardrail
compiler that converts red-team findings into runtime artifacts
across four output modalities. The library is composable, observable
through OpenTelemetry, and explicitly framed as defense in depth
rather than as a complete safety solution.

The proximal contribution is engineering: bringing CaMeL-style
capability-based IFC research to ordinary developers via drop-in
adapters for OpenAI Agents SDK, LangGraph, LlamaIndex, Anthropic
tools, and MCP. The
distal contribution is workflow: the eval-to-guardrail compiler
operationalizes a loop that has, to date, been hand-stitched in
production teams and sold as a commercial moat by Galileo and
Lakera.

The roadmap for v1.0 prioritizes: (1) replicating the headline
benchmarks (AgentDojo ASR $\leq 5\%$ at $\geq 90\%$ utility,
streaming-guard p95 added latency $\leq 80$ ms, AILuminate
time-to-fix $\leq 30$ minutes), (2) shipping pre-trained probes
per major
open-weights family, (3) hardening the MCP adapter against the next
generation of tool-poisoning attacks, and (4) delivering the audio
modality currently scoped as a stretch goal.

We invite contributions in the form of new adapters, new
\texttt{contrib/} wrappers, additional benchmark harnesses, and
documented production deployments. The repository is at
\url{https://github.com/Vidura-Wijekoon/aisafepy}.
